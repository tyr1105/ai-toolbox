## 🌟 What is AI Toolbox?

AI Toolbox is a collection of **6 free, browser-based AI tools** designed to supercharge your daily productivity. No sign-up, no paywall, no data stored — just open a link and start creating.

> **Note:** These tools are designed with **Chinese-speaking users in mind**. Interfaces, prompts, and output are optimized for Chinese content creation (with the English email writer accepting Chinese input and producing English output). International users are welcome — just be aware of the Chinese-first design philosophy!

## 🔧 The 6 Tools

| | Tool | What It Does | Best For |
|---|------|-------------|----------|
| 🎬 | [AI Video Script Writer](https://tyr1105.github.io/ai-toolbox/ai-video-script.html) | Generates viral short-video scripts in 3 seconds — hooks, storyboards, BGM cues, and shooting tips | Douyin / Kuaishou / TikTok creators |
| 📊 | [AI PPT Outline Generator](https://tyr1105.github.io/ai-toolbox/ai-ppt-outline.html) | Creates professional presentation outlines across 8 business scenarios | Reports, pitches, training decks |

## 🌍 Language & Internationalization

AI Toolbox is built **by Chinese speakers, for Chinese speakers**. Here's what that means in practice:

- **UI Language:** Simplified Chinese (zh-CN)
- **AI Prompts:** Optimized for Chinese content creation (Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Chinese workplace culture)
- **Email Writer:** Accepts Chinese input → produces native-level English output
- **API Key UI:** Prompts are in Chinese

International users are absolutely welcome to use and fork the project! The codebase is straightforward HTML/CSS/JS — easy to adapt for other languages.
